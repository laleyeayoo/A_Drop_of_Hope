# Using the prototype in interviews

A short guide for showing A Drop of Hope to patients, caregivers and specialists and learning what they need.

## Before the interview

- Open the app (web: `npm run web`, or on your phone via Expo Go) and choose **Explore with demo data**.
- Make sure nothing real is entered. The demo person, "Jordan", is fictional.
- Ask for consent to take notes. **Do not record names or identifying details** in `docs/` or anywhere in this repo.
- If you plan to publish or share findings formally, check with your university/IRB about whether the interviews
  count as human-subjects research.

## Suggested flow (20–30 minutes)

1. **Their story first (5–10 min)** — before showing anything.
   - How do you keep track of crises, medicines and appointments today? Paper, notes app, memory, a parent?
   - Tell me about your last crisis. What happened at home? At the ER?
   - What do you wish your doctors or ER staff knew about you?
2. **Show the tracker (10 min)** — hand them the phone if you can, and let them tap around.
   - "Imagine you're starting a crisis right now" → tap **I'm having a crisis**. Is this fast enough? What's missing?
   - Open a crisis from the list. Would you fill in these details later? Which ones matter?
   - Open **Medications → Hydroxyurea**. Would you rate how well a medicine is working? How often?
   - Open **Conditions & complications**. Is this list right for you? What's missing?
   - Open **Crisis treatment plan** and **Show ER card**. Would you show this at the ER? Would they use it?
   - Open **Insights**. Is any of this useful to you? To your doctor?
3. **Community (5 min)** — open the **Connect** tab.
   - Would you use a community like this? What would make you trust it?
   - Fundraisers: what has worked or not worked for you?
   - Clinical trials: have you ever looked for one? What stopped you?
4. **Privacy & research (5 min)**
   - What would you be comfortable sharing with researchers? With what protections?
   - Who would you want to be able to see your data (parent, partner, hematologist)?

## For caregivers

- Do you track for more than one person (e.g., multiple children with SCD)?
- At what age should a child/teen start managing their own tracking?

## For specialists

- What data from patients would actually help you in clinic? In research?
- How do you currently find participants for trials? Would you post studies here?
- What would make you trust patient-reported data?

## After each interview

Add anonymized notes to `docs/PLAN.md` → *Interview notes*: role (patient/caregiver/specialist), genotype if
shared, top 3 needs, top 3 pain points, and any quotes (without names). Then update the *Open questions* list.
